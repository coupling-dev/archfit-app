Check pull requests against your architecture policy

Your policy defines modules, allowed dependencies, rules and exceptions. Authors fix errors or open a request. Owners approve or reject requests.

How it works
1
Write the policy
Keep modules, allowed dependencies, rules and exceptions in .archfit.yaml.
2
Check each change
Your CI runs Archfit. The check and comment show each error and the next step.
3
Ask the owner
A request is a GitHub issue. An owner answers in Archfit; approval changes the policy by pull request.

CI checks your code. The App reads reports and policy files to show results and owner requests.

What the check says
pass
The checked change meets the policy.
fail
Fix the code, or ask, as the error says.
waiting
An open request covers the errors. Wait for the owner.
unknown
Evidence is missing or the policy differs from the protected one. This is not a pass.
