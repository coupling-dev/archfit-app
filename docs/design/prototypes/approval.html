who sees it: operators only (alexei-led, vladikk); everyone else gets 404
OptionWhat it isGood atWeak at

Why 1: the goal is protecting your GCP bill with the least effort. A queue makes each approval a conscious, one-click decision and shows the facts that matter for cost (private repositories mean trial and more uploads). Upgrade trigger: more than ~10 requests a week → add option 2's detail panel.

Architecture checks on every pull request

Archfit keeps your modules apart the way you designed them. It flags a pull request that crosses a boundary or tightens the wrong coupling, before it merges.

⏳
Your installation on octo-labs is waiting for approval.

Archfit is an experiment, and we approve installations by hand. Until then nothing runs and nothing is posted on your pull requests. Nothing to do now: this page changes once it is approved.
