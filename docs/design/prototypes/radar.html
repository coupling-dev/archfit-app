Design notes · Radar

Who it suits

Policy owners who look after several repositories, and teams with many open pull requests.

Core bet

Architecture erodes one pull request at a time. The App's home is the stream of changes and the decisions they need, not a picture of the system.

What gets easy

  • Seeing risk before the merge.
  • Finding the decisions that wait for an owner.
  • Acting on waivers before they expire.

What gets hard

  • Defining the architecture. The model is a secondary view here.
  • Trends: they need history that the App does not store.
  • Metric-watching instead of design thinking.

Data it needs

EXISTS TODAY Check runs, comments, the policy, the latest default-branch report, agent tasks.

NEW A cross-repository pull request list, the pull request report for the impact graph, and per-run summaries for trends.

Build cost

Medium in control/ui. A change in gate/feedback for the compact comment. Trends add a storage decision.

Risks and mitigations

  • Scores replace judgment: show decisions, not scores, and keep coverage and confidence next to every dimension.
  • Noise: order the inbox by what needs a person.
